About Sepik Logistics

Local transport information in one place.

Our mission

Many people in the Sepik region cannot easily find out what transport is available, how much it costs or when it leaves. Sepik Logistics puts that information in one place and makes it simple to ask for a quote.

Who we serve

  • Villages and families sending or receiving goods
  • Small businesses and trade stores
  • NGOs and church organisations
  • Schools and health centres
