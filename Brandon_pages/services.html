Our services

Four transport modes, one simple way to book.

What we carry and how

Road freight

Regular trucks between Wewak and the main inland towns.

Typical cargo: store goods, building materials, produce.

Best for: towns on the highway and anything heavy or bulky.

River transport

Boats along the Sepik River and its tributaries.

Typical cargo: supplies for villages, fuel, passengers, market goods.

Best for: communities without road access.

Sea freight

Coastal vessels linking Wewak with other ports.

Typical cargo: containers, vehicles, large shipments.

Best for: large, non-urgent loads over long distances.

Air cargo

Flights from Wewak and Vanimo to Port Moresby and regional airstrips.

Typical cargo: medicine, documents, small urgent parcels.

Best for: fast delivery of light items.
